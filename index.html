Morning
Take medication
First thing — before coffee, before checking your phone. Put it next to your water glass tonight so it's the first thing you see.
Feed the cats
Morning feeding. Anchoring this to your morning routine makes one less thing to remember.
10–15 min morning tidy
Before you sit down to work — clear surfaces, put things away, straighten cushions. A cluttered space measurably raises baseline stress. Ten minutes now saves the late-evening "I should clean" loop.
Make your bed
Sets the tone for the entire day. One completed task before anything else — the simplest momentum builder there is.
10 min morning sunlight
Outside within the first hour of waking — no sunglasses. Anchors your cortisol curve and circadian rhythm. Directly reduces nighttime grinding.
16 oz water before coffee
You wake up dehydrated. Rehydrate first — coffee before water spikes cortisol on an empty, dry system.
First
Morning skincare
Cleanse + moisturize + SPF. Sun protection is the single highest-ROI skincare step — especially in Denver at altitude where UV is 25% stronger.
SPF — face and neck
Apply SPF 30+ to your face, neck, and any exposed skin before going outside. The most impactful anti-aging habit you can build in your 20s. Takes 30 seconds.
Vitamin D with breakfast
Fat-soluble — take with food. Deficiency is linked to bruxism, poor sleep, and low mood. Especially important in Denver winters with reduced UV.
Jaw exercise — morning
Three rounds of two minutes (default — you can adjust). Use the timer below and follow the specific sequence your dentist gave you.
6 min
Morning posture reset
Run through the checklist below before sitting down. The goal is to make the correct posture your default — repetition rewires it.
10 sec
Quick reset
  • Sit bones grounded — not slumped onto your tailbone
  • Ribs over hips — stack, don't splay
  • Shoulders dropped back and down from ears
  • Chin tucked — ears over shoulders, not in front
  • Jaw check — lips together, teeth apart, tongue to roof
Midday
Jaw exercise — midday
Three rounds of two minutes. The midday session catches accumulated tension from morning concentration before it compounds.
6 min
Stand break + posture reset
Stand up for 2 minutes — walk to get water, look out a window. When you sit back down, run through the standing checklist below.
2 min
Standing reset
  • Weight even across both feet, hip-width apart
  • Soft knee bend — never locked
  • Hips neutral — lightly engage lower abs
  • Chest open, string pulling crown of head up
  • Jaw check — unclench right now
Afternoon
20-20-20 eye break
Every 20 minutes of screen time, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Reduces eye strain that often shows up as headaches and jaw tension. Set a recurring phone reminder to start.
No caffeine after 2pm
Caffeine has a 5–6 hour half-life. Afternoon coffee at 3pm still has half its effect at 8–9pm — disrupting sleep architecture and directly worsening grinding.
Afternoon posture check
By 3pm most people are slumped. Reset your sitting position and check the desk/couch fix list below — small adjustments now prevent end-of-day pain.
10 sec
Desk / couch fix
  • Screen at eye level — prop it if it's below
  • Elbows at 90° — not reaching or winged
  • Low back support — pillow at lumbar if on couch
  • Don't cross your legs — rotates your pelvis
  • Jaw check — are you tensing your face right now?
30 min reading
Screens off, book or e-reader only. Reading reduces stress cortisol by up to 68% in research — more than walking or listening to music. Schedule it like a meeting.

Breathing practice
4-7-8: inhale 4s · hold 7s · exhale 8s. Or box breathing: 4s each side. All patterns shift your nervous system toward parasympathetic. Use the breathing timer above. Nasal breathing only — mouth breathing during sleep increases grinding severity.
